## Plan

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-pmk-4-days-off-design.md`
Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-25-pmk-4-days-off.md`

1. The tracker validates and stores days off per member in the sprint file (`days_off`). Dates must be real `YYYY-MM-DD` weekdays inside the sprint, the member must exist, and a date cannot repeat. The contract gains `getDaysOff` and `updateCycleCapacity`.
2. `cycle capacity <id> --days-off ana=2026-10-16+2026-10-19` sets one member's list, and `ana=none` clears it. `--set` and `--days-off` work alone or together in one write. The read JSON becomes `{ capacity, days_off }`.
3. `/pmkit:cycle:plan` stores the days off it asks for with the new flag.
