## Summary

PR: https://github.com/fredsvanelli/pmkit/pull/100

- Customers live in `.issues/customers/<ID>.md` and requests in `.issues/requests/<ID>.md`. The IDs are `CUS-` and `REQ-` with a code that follows `id_style`.
- New CLI commands: `customers list|show|create`, `requests list|get|create|accept|decline` and `demand list`. The JSON uses the keys of `linear customers list` and `show`.
- A request starts `open`. Accept or decline triages it and records the state, the issue, the reason and who triaged it when. A new issue comes from `issues create` followed by `requests accept`.
- `demand_signals` and `request_queue` are native on Local. On Linear they stay `absent`, with `detail.workspace` and `detail.unsupported`.
- `pmkit validate`, the doctor and the export to Linear cover both folders. The tracker format stays 2.
