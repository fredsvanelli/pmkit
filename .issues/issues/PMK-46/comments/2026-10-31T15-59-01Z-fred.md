## Plan

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-31-pmk-46-customers-and-requests-design.md`. Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-31-pmk-46-customers-and-requests.md`.

1. File formats. `.issues/customers/<ID>.md` (name, status, tier, revenue, size, owner, notes) and `.issues/requests/<ID>.md` (title, customer, from, important, created, state, issue, reason, triaged, triaged_by, body). IDs are `CUS-` and `REQ-` with a code that follows `id_style`. The records use the keys of Linear's `customerView` and `requestView`.
2. Tracker operations: `listCustomers`, `getCustomer`, `createCustomer`, `listRequests`, `getRequest`, `createRequest`, `acceptRequest`, `declineRequest`, `listDemandSignals`. A request is `open`, `accepted` (into an issue) or `declined`. A broken file is skipped and reported in `problems`. Linear stubs refuse with the manifest hint.
3. CLI: `customers list|show|create`, `requests list|get|create|accept|decline`, `demand list`. `customers list` and `customers show` print the JSON keys of `linear customers list` and `show`. `demand_signals` and `request_queue` become native on Local and stay `absent` on Linear, with `detail.workspace` and `detail.unsupported`.
4. `pmkit validate` checks both folders and their references, the doctor lists the new problem kinds, the export to Linear lists customers and requests among the losses, and README, CHANGELOG, CONTEXT, the design spec and ADR-0001 get updated.

Decisions: fixed `CUS` and `REQ` prefixes; the tracker format stays 2; a new issue comes from `issues create` followed by `requests accept`; a request without a customer counts as demand through its `from`.
