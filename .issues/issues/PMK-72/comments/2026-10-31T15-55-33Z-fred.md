1. Add scripts/lib/team-capacity.mjs (parse pairs, merge capacity, working days, member check, change list) and turn runTeamLoad into a shared teamLoadReport.
2. Add `pmkit team capacity [--sprint current|id] [--set member=n|none] [--days-off member=date+date|none]`. On Linear it writes only .pmkit/capacity.yaml and says so in a note.
3. Add commands/team/capacity.md with a full preview for more than one member, a scenario, the count updates, and the README, SKILL.md and CHANGELOG lines.

The acceptance criterion is covered by steps 2 and 3.
