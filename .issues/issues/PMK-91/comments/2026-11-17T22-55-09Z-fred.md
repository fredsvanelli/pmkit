Done in PR #317: https://github.com/fredsvanelli/pmkit/pull/317

- `/pmkit:github:draft-issues` (`commands/github/draft-issues.md`) reviews the drafts of a Project with a maturity note, shows a full preview, converts the chosen drafts, then offers one batched `issues update` preview for type, priority and labels.
- `github draft-issues list` reads `tracker.scope.project` or a linked `--project`, and returns the unarchived drafts with their field values (`available: false` with no Project).
- `github draft-issues convert <item...>` runs `convertProjectV2DraftIssueItemToIssue`, so the Project item keeps its fields. Its dry run checks every item and the target first, and one wrong item converts nothing. `--repo` sends drafts to another repository, with a warning that pmkit does not track it.
- The capability manifest does not change. GHES 3.19 has everything used.
- Design `docs/specs/2026-11-17-pmk-91-github-draft-issues-design.md`, plan `docs/plans/2026-11-17-pmk-91-github-draft-issues.md`, runbook `docs/runbooks/draft-issues-github.md` with its E2E test, and the CONTEXT.md term "Draft issue".
