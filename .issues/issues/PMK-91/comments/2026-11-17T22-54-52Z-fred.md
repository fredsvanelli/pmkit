Plan for PMK-91, from the approved design `docs/specs/2026-11-17-pmk-91-github-draft-issues-design.md` and the plan `docs/plans/2026-11-17-pmk-91-github-draft-issues.md`:

1. `github draft-issues list [--project owner/number] [--limit n]`: pages the Project's items, keeps the unarchived drafts with their field values. `tracker.scope.project` by default, or a Project linked to the repository.
2. `github draft-issues convert <item...> [--project] [--repo owner/name]`: a dry run checks every item and the target, then `convertProjectV2DraftIssueItemToIssue` runs per draft. One wrong item converts nothing, and a failure partway names what converted.
3. `/pmkit:github:draft-issues`: review with a maturity note, a full preview, the conversion, then one optional `issues update` preview for type, priority and labels of the new issues in `tracker.scope.repo`.
4. Tests against the stateful fake GitHub, and the runbook `docs/runbooks/draft-issues-github.md` with its E2E test.
