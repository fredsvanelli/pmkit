Plan for PMK-47. Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-31-pmk-47-user-inbox-design.md`. Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-31-pmk-47-user-inbox.md`.

1. A mention finder and two user store functions. The check lives in `inbox/<repo-hash>.json`, never in `.issues/`.
2. Contract operations `listNotifications` and `markRead` on the Local tracker. Items come from `assign` and `create` events and from `@member` in comments and descriptions. A file that does not parse makes the inbox incomplete and blocks mark-read.
3. CLI `inbox list` and `inbox mark-read` with a stable `--json`. `user_inbox` becomes native on Local and stays `absent` on Linear.
4. README, CHANGELOG, SKILL.md, glossary and ADR-0001.
