## Summary

PR: https://github.com/fredsvanelli/pmkit/pull/104

- `inbox list` shows the assignments and `@member` mentions newer than the
member's last check. `inbox mark-read` records the check in the user store (`inbox/<repo-hash>.json`), never in `.issues/`.
- Assignments come from `assign` events and from issues created with the member as assignee, by someone else. Mentions come from comments and descriptions, outside code and emails, not by the member.
- A file that does not parse makes the result `complete: false`, with a warning and `problems`. `mark-read` refuses until it is fixed.
- `user_inbox` is native on Local and `absent` on Linear.
