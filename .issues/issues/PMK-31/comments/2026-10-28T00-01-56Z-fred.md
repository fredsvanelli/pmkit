What changed:
- The adapter registry in `scripts/lib/adapters.mjs` holds only `local`. A hosted system with no adapter still fails with `NOT_SUPPORTED`.
- `loadPmkitConfig` reads `.pmkit/config.yaml` for a hosted tracker and does not open `.issues/`. The schema and `validatePmkitConfig` check `tracker.scope`, `mappings.status` and `mappings.fields`.
- Commands use a frozen contract-only view. Local-only features go through `ctx.local()`, and the CLI awaits every contract call.
- A fake hosted adapter that returns promises runs the commands in `scripts/cli-hosted.test.mjs`.

`npm test` passes (1523 tests) and `validate` reports no problems.
