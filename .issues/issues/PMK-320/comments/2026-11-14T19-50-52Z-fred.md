Plan for PMK-320 (spec and plan in `docs/superpowers/` on the branch).

Approach: mirror the HTML guard. In place, `fenceRisk` names a fence only when it is the top-level fence still open at the end of the `sectionEnd` walk (`held.openFence`, like `held.unclosed` for HTML). A fence in a quote or list item is never open at the end of the section: its container ends at the blank line, the heading, a later line or the end of the file. This replaces PMK-317's `dropped`/`ended` bookkeeping, so there is one way to find the end of a container.

1. Tests first: move the PMK-314 and PMK-317 cases that pin the refusal for a container that ends at the heading, at the blank line before it, at a setext heading or at the end of the file to the in-place list, with the reason (CommonMark ends the fence with its container; the in-place write keeps every line). Keep the refusals: top-level fence that does not close, whole-section replacement (status in item or quote, no known status line, two status lines, status after the fence).
2. Implement in `scripts/lib/adr.mjs` (`sectionEnd`, `fenceRisk`).
3. Proof: develop x branch on random Status bodies against commonmark.js 0.31.2, plus `scripts/dev/adr-commonmark-diff.mjs` in full (0 worse), both in the PR.
4. Docs: `commands/spec/adr-status.md`, ADR 0006, README, CHANGELOG.
