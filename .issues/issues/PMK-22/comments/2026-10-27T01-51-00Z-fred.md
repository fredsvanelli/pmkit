Releases now work in the Local tracker. A release is a file under `releases/`, and an issue joins one through a `release` field.

- The CLI has `release create`, `list`, `get`, `issues` and `mark-released`, and `issues create` and `update` take `--release`.
- `validate` and `/pmkit:local:doctor` check release files and references.
- `pmkit capabilities` reports `releases` as `native`. The tracker format stays at 2.
- Tests: `npm test` passes, 1182 of 1182. `validate` reports no problems.
