Plan for PMK-221 (spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-pmk-221-customers-and-requests-improvements-design.md`, plan `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-10-pmk-221-customers-and-requests-improvements.md`).

1. `customers update <id or name>` (name, status, tier, revenue, size, owner; `none` clears status, tier, owner) on Local, as a new contract operation `updateCustomer`. Frontmatter lines are edited in place, so comments and unknown keys stay.
2. `requests update <id> [--title | --title-file] [--body-file]` on Local (`updateRequest`), title and body only; `triaged` does not change.
3. `requests accept <id> --create`: creates the issue (request title; body plus `Asked by:` and `Request:` lines) and accepts the request into it. An accepted request is refused with CONFLICT. When the accept fails after the create, the error names both IDs and the next step.
4. Linear serves `listCustomers`, `getCustomer`, `createCustomer`, `updateCustomer`, `createRequest` and `listDemandSignals` on top of `linear customers`, with the Local record shapes (a Linear request's title is the first line of its body). `demand_signals` becomes native on Linear with customers on, so `prioritize-by-demand` uses customer requests there. The request queue (Asks) stays unsupported on Linear, so `request-queue` keeps stopping at step 1 there.
5. `customers show` takes the customer as a positional or as `--customer`, on both trackers, with one JSON shape.
6. Docs: prioritize-by-demand step 1, linear customers, README CLI table, CHANGELOG.

Builder's calls are marked in the spec.
