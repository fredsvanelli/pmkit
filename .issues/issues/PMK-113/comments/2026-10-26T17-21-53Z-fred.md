## Plan

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-26-pmk-113-split-original-design.md`
Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-26-pmk-113-split-original.md`

The decision: after a split in `/pmkit:cycle:close`, the original moves to `done` and stays in the closed sprint with its estimate lowered to the delivered part. The new backlog issue gets a `related` link to it. The split already lowers the estimate to what was delivered, so `done` says what the command meant. `/pmkit:cycle:plan` already offers the new issue (`--category todo,backlog --sprint none`), so `plan.md` does not change. Nothing is deleted, and no CLI or tracker code changes.

Options weighed: `canceled` (loses the delivered points and reads as abandoned), carry over with no split (plan never offers an `in_progress` issue), a reverse split (more writes, one with no dry run, and the original stays `in_progress`).

1. `commands/cycle/close.md` steps 6 to 8 and the `cycle-close` scenario. A split runs only when the delivered part is above 0 and below the original's estimate. With no estimate, no split is offered. Step 7 dry-runs `issues move <id> done`, and its preview lists the new issue, the link, the new estimate, the move, and what stays on the original (open blockers, open sub-issues, unchecked criteria). Step 8 runs create, link, estimate, move, then reads `cycle issues` again for the delivered numbers.
2. The rest of `close.md` (step 9, Output, "If something is missing") and the PMK-10 exception list for links that have no dry run.
3. A CLI test that guards plan's query after a split.

Known limit: velocity counts the delivered points only when the close runs on or before the sprint's end date, the same gap `pr sync` has today. Follow-up candidate.
