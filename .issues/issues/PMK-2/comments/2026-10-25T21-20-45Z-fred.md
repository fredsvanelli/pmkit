PMK-2 is ready for review. `pmkit capabilities` now reports the 8 capabilities that no command serves as `absent`, each with an `enableHint`. Before, they came back as native or derived. The Local tracker serves 12 of 20: 10 native and 2 derived.
Tests: `npm test` passes 424 of 424.
Criterion 1 (native or derived means served): `SERVED_BY` in `scripts/lib/capabilities.mjs` lists each command. Two tests check it: the command names exist in the CLI table, and each native or derived capability has at least one command.
Criterion 2 (absent with enableHint): `capabilities --json` returns 8 absent entries and every one has an enableHint.
Criterion 3 (test fails on a native or derived capability without a command): `unservedCapabilities` flags a doctored `releases` marked native, and a test covers it.
Criterion 4 (ADR-0001 lists what is served): the ADR now lists the 10 native, 2 derived and 8 absent capabilities.
