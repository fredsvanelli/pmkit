Stage 6 is done. The work is in PR https://github.com/fredsvanelli/pmkit/pull/63.

What changed:

- Linear issue history becomes events in the Local shape. Old values come from rewinding the current issue, and replaying the events gives back `getIssue`.
- Status events use the category of each state through the mappings.
- `metricEvents` works on Linear, so the flow metrics read Linear the way they read Local.
- Comments are listed, added and replied to in threads.
- A new contract operation resolves a thread. It is built on Local first, then on Linear, with four new goldens for `issues resolve`.
- A read-only live check replayed 10 issues of the sandbox workspace and matched `getIssue`.

Comment mutations are proven on the fake only. PMK-39 covers the live tests.
