Done: PR #313, https://github.com/fredsvanelli/pmkit/pull/313 (base `develop`).

- `/pmkit:github:discussions` lists the discussions of the repository, summarizes one in six parts (header, question, positions, agreement, open questions, outcome so far), turns it into an issue through `issues create` with one confirmation, then offers a `Tracked in #<issue>` comment on the discussion. It never closes or locks a discussion.
- Three GitHub-only CLI calls: `github discussions list`, `github discussions get <number>`, `github discussions comment <number> --body-file f`. They read through GraphQL, which GHES 3.19 has in full, and answer `available: false` when Discussions are off. The capability manifest does not change.
- Docs: README, help, SKILL.md, CONTEXT.md (the term Discussion), CHANGELOG, the design, the plan and the runbook `docs/runbooks/discussions-github.md`, checked by `scripts/runbook-discussions-github.test.mjs`.
