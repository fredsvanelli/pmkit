A full `npm test` leaves about 347 `pmkit-*` folders in the temp folder today, from 12 test files. The biggest are `cli` (62), `cli-pr` (44) and `sprints` (41).

1. `scripts/test-helpers.mjs` gets `tempDir(prefix)`, a registry that a root `afterEach` empties after every test, pass or fail, with a process `exit` fallback. `makeRepo` and `fakeGh` create their folders through it, so their 158 callers do not change.
2. Every hand-written `mkdtempSync` in the tests goes through `tempDir`.
3. `npm test` runs `scripts/run-tests.mjs`. It runs the same `node --test` glob with a private `TMPDIR`, fails and lists the folders when anything is left, and removes the private folder at the end.
