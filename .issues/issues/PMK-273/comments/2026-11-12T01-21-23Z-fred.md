In `scripts/lib/adr.mjs`, `locate().end` ends the `## Status` section at the first line (outside fenced code, after the `## Status` line) that is an ATX heading `^ {0,3}#{1,6}([ \t]|$)` (indented, tab after `#`, empty) or the first line of a paragraph closed by a setext underline `=+` or `-+`.
