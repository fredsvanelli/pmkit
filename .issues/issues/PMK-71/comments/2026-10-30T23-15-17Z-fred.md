Plan for PMK-71. Spec: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-30-pmk-71-team-load-design.md. Plan: docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-30-pmk-71-team-load-command.md.

1. A pure module, scripts/lib/team-load.mjs, computes issues, points, work in progress, oldest work in progress, imbalances (over 1.5 times the average, over capacity), members with no work and move suggestions. PMK-72 can reuse it.
2. A read-only CLI command, `pmkit team load [--sprint]`, reads the active sprint (or every open issue when there are no sprints), capacity, days off and aging, and prints a table.
3. `/pmkit:team:load` shows the result and reassigns with `issues assign` after a full preview that always asks.

Criterion 1 is covered by steps 1 and 2. Criterion 2 is covered by step 3. Criterion 3 is covered by the unit choice, which prints a `note:` when it counts issues. Agent members are listed but stay out of the balance and the reassign targets.
