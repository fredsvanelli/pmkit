Added `/pmkit:backlog:triage` (`commands/backlog/triage.md`). It lists the triage queue with a proposed priority, labels, duplicate or decline for each issue, and applies the accepted decisions after one preview.
- Accepted issues move to `backlog`. Declined issues move to `canceled` with a reason comment. Duplicates get a `duplicate` link and move to `canceled`.
- No CLI change. A scenario covers apply all, one by one and an empty queue. `npm test` passes (1102 of 1102).
- The version and the changelog stay for the release.
