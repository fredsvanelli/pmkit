Summary of the work on PMK-37.

Linear cycles now map to pmkit sprints. The sprint id is the cycle number.

- Listing sprints, finding the current one, listing its issues and moving issues are native.
- Capacity and days off live in the committed `.pmkit/capacity.yaml`, checked by the Local validators.
- Opening and closing a sprint answer `NOT_SUPPORTED` with the reason. They send no request.
- The contract suite gained a `cycle-lifecycle` family and a cycle provider. The Local run is unchanged.
