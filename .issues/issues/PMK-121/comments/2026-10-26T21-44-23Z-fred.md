Decision (rule A): `cycle close` records a `closed` timestamp on the sprint, and velocity counts a move to `done` up to it. A closed sprint without `closed` keeps the end-date rule, so old sprints keep their numbers and no file is rewritten. `/pmkit:cycle:close` step 8 now runs the moves and the split before the close, so a split original counts.

All four criteria are proven: a late close in a temp project gives 3 issues and 10 points in both the close report and `metrics velocity` (the call `/pmkit:cycle:plan` makes), a move after the close does not count, a sprint without `closed` keeps the old count, and `npm test` passes 1087 of 1087.
