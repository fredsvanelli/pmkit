## Plan

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-25-pmk-3-milestones-design.md`
Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-25-pmk-3-milestones.md`

1. Add `scripts/lib/local/milestones.mjs`. A milestone lives in `milestones/<id>.md` with a `name` (defaults to the id) and an optional `due` date. The tracker gains `listMilestones`, `getMilestone` and `createMilestone`, and the contract suite covers them.
2. Check milestone references on issue and epic writes and in `validate`, but only once `milestones/` holds a `.md` file. Trackers from 0.2.0 that use free-text milestone names keep working.
3. Add `pmkit milestone list`, `milestone get <id>` and `milestone create <id> [--name] [--due]`. `milestone create` warns about milestone names that issues or epics use without a file.
4. `/pmkit:spec:import` creates the milestones of its tree, and `/pmkit:local:doctor` proposes `milestone create` for names without a file. Update the design spec, CONTEXT.md and README.

The tracker format stays 2, with no migration. The capability manifest does not change: `releases` belongs to PMK-22.
