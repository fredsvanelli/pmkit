Added `/pmkit:issues:dependencies <id>` and the read-only CLI command `issues dependencies <id>`. For an issue or an epic, it shows the hierarchy, the chain of open blockers and the critical path. It falls back to the hierarchy only, with a reason, when no open blocker touches the target.
The pure module is `scripts/lib/dependencies.mjs`. Loops in hand-made `blocked_by` links are broken with a warning, and missing or unreadable blockers stay in the chain.
`npm test` passes (1141 of 1141), and `validate` reports no problems. The command writes nothing.
